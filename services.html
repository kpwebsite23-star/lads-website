HEALTH & PROPERTY BEAUTY

Tree Trimming, Thinning & Canopy Pruning

Proper pruning is essential for tree longevity, storm resistance, and aesthetic beauty. We prune according to ANSI A300 arborist standards to promote structural integrity and protect your investment.

Overgrown branches reduce sunlight to your lawn, scrape roof shingles, and trap high winds like a sail during severe weather. Our precision canopy pruning elevates clearance, thins interior deadwood, and balances branch weight to prevent future catastrophic splits.

Tree Trimming Highlights & Capabilities:

  • Canopy Lifting & Crown Thinning: Improves sunlight penetration for healthy turf and reduces wind resistance during storms.
  • Deadwood & Hazard Elimination: Systematic removal of decaying, diseased, dying, or rubbing branches before they fail.
  • Roof, Siding & Utility Clearance: Prunes limbs at least 6–10 feet back from roofs, chimneys, siding, driveways, and service lines.
  • Species-Specific Aesthetic Shaping: Custom pruning for Kansas pin oaks, silver maples, elms, evergreens, and ornamental flowering trees.

LAWN RESTORATION

Complete Below-Grade Stump Grinding

Tree stumps are unsightly trip hazards that attract termites, carpenter ants, and fungi while impeding mowing and lawn care. We grind stumps deep below grade to fully restore your property.

Our powerful commercial grinding equipment pulverizes stumps and chases expansive surface root systems without tearing up your turf. We can easily access backyards through standard 36-inch gates and leave the ground level and ready for fresh sod, topsoil, or new landscape plantings.

Stump Grinding Highlights & Capabilities:

  • Deep Grinding (6–12+ Inches Below Grade): Completely eliminates the root plate so trees will never re-sprout, allowing immediate replanting.
  • Surface Root Flare Chasing: Grinds prominent, exposed surface roots extending across your lawn to protect lawnmower blades.
  • Compact Gate-Access Machinery: Lightweight, high-torque grinders that maneuver through standard backyard gates without rutting turf.
  • Mulch Management & Backfill Options: Choose between clean backfilling with nutrient-rich wood chips or complete debris haul-away.
